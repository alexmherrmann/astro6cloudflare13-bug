# Welcome to Astro

Astro is a modern static site generator that lets you build faster websites with less JavaScript.

## Why Astro?

- **Zero JavaScript by default** - Ship HTML with no client-side JS unless you need it
- **Component Islands** - Add interactivity only where needed
- **Framework agnostic** - Use React, Vue, Svelte, or just HTML

## Content Collections

Astro's content collections provide a type-safe way to manage your content. You define a schema, and Astro validates your frontmatter automatically.

This makes it easy to:

1. Keep your content organized
2. Catch errors early
3. Get autocomplete in your editor
